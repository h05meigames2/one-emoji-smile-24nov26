One emoji, chosen to make a stranger smile: 🫶

## Why 🫶 (heart hands)

Heart hands are a small, deliberate gesture — two hands forming a heart — rather than a face. They read as warm and friendly at first glance, need no context, and are understood the same way by a stranger of any age or background. They also feel *chosen*: not the first smiley that comes to mind (no 🙂, no 😊), but a gesture someone picked on purpose. And they fit a stranger perfectly — affectionate without intimacy, playful without sarcasm or flirtation.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes exactly one emoji to `/output/entry.txt`, UTF-8, no network, deterministic.
